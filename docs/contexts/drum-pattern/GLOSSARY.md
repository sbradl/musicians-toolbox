# Drum Pattern

Writing snare and bass drum patterns in drum notation and practicing them.

## Language

**Pattern**:
A snare and bass drum exercise of one or more bars in one Time signature.
_Avoid_: Groove, exercise, score

**Beat**:
One counted slot of a bar, holding the Notes its note value says.
_Avoid_: Onset, count, pulse

**Note**:
One position inside a Beat: the snare stroke and its hand, whether the bass drum plays, its accent.
_Avoid_: Hit, step
